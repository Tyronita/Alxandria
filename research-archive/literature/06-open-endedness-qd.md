# Chapter 6 — Open-endedness and quality diversity (draft skeleton)

**Citation keys**: `stanley2017open`, `lehman2011novelty`, `mouret2015illuminating`, `pugh2016quality`, `wang2019paired`, `ecoffet2019go`, `oudeyer2007intrinsic`.

## Concepts

- **Novelty search**: optimize novelty metric instead of task fitness (`lehman2011novelty`).
- **MAP-Elites**: maintain best performer per niche in descriptor space (`mouret2015illuminating`).
- **POET-style co-evolution**: coupled environments and agents (`wang2019paired`).

## Failure modes

Deceptive landscapes; collapse of diversity; **interestingness** dependence on observer (`hughes2024open`).

## Example box

Run toy MAP-Elites on a 2D behavior descriptor with known elite placements—manual arithmetic.

## Figure hooks

Grid archives + novelty radial plots (`pugh2016quality`).
